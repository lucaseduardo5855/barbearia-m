# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners, administrators, and employees of barbershops use the administrative panel during the workday to operate their unit.

## Product Purpose

The product lets barbershops manage bookings, staff, services, products, financial activity, and their public profile from one workspace.

## Operating Context

Work is organized per barbershop unit. Owners and administrators access all management areas; employees have access limited to the operational areas relevant to their role.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The panel includes overview, agenda, booking history, financial records, commissions, reports, services, inventory, team, marketing, and configuration. Existing data, Portuguese content, role permissions, and product workflows must be preserved.

## Brand Commitments

The administrative visual system should follow the supplied Shadcn Next.js AdminCN template, while retaining the barbershop's real content and data.

## Product Principles

- Keep daily operation fast to scan and easy to act on.
- Preserve the existing business data and workflows.
- Make the current unit and role-aware navigation unambiguous.
- Use a consistent, accessible component language across every administrative area.
